🔥 How to use Phoenix
🟠 Orange = WHO? — who is in the sentence?
🟡 Yellow = WHAT DOING? — what are they doing? Use "I am", "he is" etc. for correct grammar!
🔵 Blue = WHERE? — where does it happen?
🟢 Green = WHAT? — what object or thing?
🔴 Red = WHAT LIKE? — describing words & feelings
🟣 Purple = HOW? — in what way?
🩷 Pink = WHO TO? — who receives?
🩵 Teal = WHEN? — what time?
👆 Tap any coloured word to add it to your sentence
✏️ Type your own word in the text box above the colour bar
